PSIPHON PROVIDER
================

Nivan Shield never downloads or executes an unverified Psiphon binary.

To enable this provider, obtain the official Windows ConsoleClient and its
client.config from the Psiphon project. Place them here as:

  ConsoleClient.exe
  client.config

Then open Nivan Shield > Psiphon, select both files, and approve the
publisher/fingerprint. The SHA-256 fingerprint is pinned and checked again
before every launch. Do not use binaries received from Telegram channels,
file-sharing sites, or unknown mirrors.
